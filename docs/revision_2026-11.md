## Phase B: hardware, the two review items that need the arm

The operator is Luai. Nothing in this phase touches the robot without him at the bench. Everything
that can be prepared without the robot is prepared this week so the bench time is spent on trials.

### B0. Diagnose the recurring gripper overload (prepare now, test at the bench)

Seed 2 was stopped by it, seed 1 lost a trajectory to it, the history-control session died with it
plus a displaced camera. Until it is understood, every new session is at risk.
- Prepare: a diagnostic script that reads the STS3215 status registers of the gripper servo
  (present load, current, temperature, voltage, the overload / protection flags, the configured
  torque and current limits) at rest, under a held grasp, and after a scripted close-on-object, and
  logs them. Compare the gripper's configured limits with the other joints.
- Candidates to test at the bench: the jaw closing on the adapter at full command with the vendor
  torque limit (sustained stall current), a loose horn or binding jaw, supply sag at 12 V under
  stall, a firmware protection threshold that resets on power cycle. Record what was found in
  `docs/hardware/overload_diagnosis_2026-11.md`. If the fix changes the plant (new torque limit,
  new jaw stop), say so and treat later sessions as a new plant version in the records.

### B1. Finish the pre-registered position-history control (highest scientific value)

`docs/hardware/preregistration_2026-09-07_history_control.md` already registers the two paired
comparisons: tracking error vs position history at seed 1, and position only vs position history at
seed 0, 20 pairs each. 9 pairs of the first exist. This answers the question the paper currently
concedes ("position history recovers the gain, so what is new?"), and it is also reviewer item 2.
- Checkpoints `ghist_s0`, `ghist_s1` exist under `checkpoints/grid_G_ghist_s1/` and the replication
  folder; verify hashes against the pre-evaluation manifest and offline observation parity before
  the session.
- Decide and write down before the session: whether the 9 existing pairs are pooled with the new
  ones (the pre-registration governs; if it is silent, run 20 fresh pairs and report the 9
  separately).
- Blind the labels: adopt the flagship's practice (`~/projects/contact-insertion/scripts/hw/
  label_trials.py` idea): record a video per trial and label success from the video afterwards,
  ideally by someone who did not run the trial. Add a camera recording hook to
  `trial_runner.py` if it is not there. This removes the "operator knows the policy" caveat.

### B2. Seeds so seed 1 does not carry the result (reviewer item 2)

After B0, either complete seed 2 to 20 pairs (its 6 pairs stay reported as the interrupted record;
the new pairs are a new versioned session) or train and evaluate a seed 3 with the frozen recipe.
Training needs no operator and can run overnight this week on the Orin (2 cells, roughly 1 to 2 h

### B3. Grasp-state input (reviewer item 1)

Train position-only + binary grasp bit, and tracking-error + binary grasp bit, at one seed, same
recipe. Define the bit from the recorded jaw position threshold used in `demonstration_summary.json`
(grasp-closure frame), document it, and evaluate both against their counterparts in 20 pairs each.
If the grasp bit recovers the tracking-error gain, the paper says so; that is a result, not a
failure. Pre-register before training.

Bench budget: each 20-pair session is 40 rollouts of about 14 s plus manual resets, roughly 2 h with
setup. B1 is two sessions, B2 one, B3 two. Order of value: B0, B1, B2, B3. First bench dates are on
Luai's calendar for Sat Nov 21 and Sun Nov 22, after his TCF exam on Nov 20; training and all
