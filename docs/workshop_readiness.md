## Research claim

Adding tracking error improves the completed paired hardware comparisons
under this task and execution protocol. Simulation history controls leave
the preferred temporal representation unresolved. The work does not establish
force regulation, superiority over hardware position history, or reliability
across tasks and retraining.

The practical contribution is a controlled example of how observation
history, command limiting, and actuator telemetry affect what can be inferred
from a robot-learning result. The filled reporting table in the supplement
records those choices and distinguishes nominal from achieved timing.
