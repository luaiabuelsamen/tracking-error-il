Research question

What does the command leave out?

The gap between a commanded joint position and the measured position contains information about actuator response. I study whether giving that tracking error to an imitation policy helps it manipulate objects.

Real teleoperation, autonomous execution, and simulation are labelled separately. Showcase footage illustrates behavior; the recorded evaluation counts below are the evidence.
